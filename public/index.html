        How to read this & sources

        mpd = miles per S$1 at each card's standard airline-partner rate (HSBC: 4 mpd; KrisFlyer converts at 30,000:10,000 since 16 Jan 2025, so 3.33 – shown in notes). Enter an amount and cards are ranked by total miles: the bonus rate up to the cap still left this cycle, then the base rate, with the minimum spend and any earn block applied. unverified marks rules not confirmed by the bank or MileLion. approximated marks a total that skips a cap or uses a rounding rule that is not in the issuer's T&Cs. FCY in-store assumes a contactless tap (phone wallet for UOB PV). In-app Apple/Google Pay = Online.
